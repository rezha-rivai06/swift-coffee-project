Our Story

Crafting Comfort Since 2012

Cafe Bunda began with a simple dream: to create a space that feels like your mother's living room. We believe coffee isn't just a drink; it's an invitation to pause, connect, and belong.

Our beans are ethically sourced from local Indonesian farmers, roasted in small batches to preserve their unique profiles, and served by baristas who know your name and your regular order.
